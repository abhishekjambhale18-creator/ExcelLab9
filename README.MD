# 📊 Excel — Sales Dashboard & Data Analysis

## 📌 Description

Performed **sales data analysis and dashboard creation** in **Microsoft Excel** using the DMart Retail Business Dataset. The assigned tasks involved summarizing sales, revenue, profit, regional performance, customer types, categories, and other business metrics using Pivot Tables and data analysis techniques.

## 🧩 Analysis Performed

**01 — Sales Analysis**  
Analyzed total sales across different regions, categories, cities, months, and years.

**02 — Profit Analysis**  
Summarized total profit by region and analyzed average profit across cities and product categories.

**03 — Revenue Analysis**  
Calculated and presented the overall sales and net revenue generated from the dataset.

**04 — Customer & Payment Analysis**  
Analyzed sales by customer type and examined transaction counts across different payment modes.

**05 — Category & Regional Analysis**  
Compared average sales across product categories and regions to understand their performance.

**06 — Pivot Table Analysis**  
Used Pivot Tables to summarize and organize different aspects of the DMart sales data.

**07 — Dashboard Creation**  
Created a **D-Mart Retail Business Dashboard** to present key metrics and provide a summarized view of the analysis.
